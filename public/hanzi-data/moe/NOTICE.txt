This local corpus is provided only for noncommercial teaching use. The
normalized JSON preserves the official stroke order, stroke outlines, and
ordered writing tracks. It applies one technical coordinate-format conversion
to every character so the official data can be consumed by Hanzi Writer:

  x' = x / 2
  y' = 900 - y / 2
